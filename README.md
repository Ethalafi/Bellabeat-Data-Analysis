# Bellabeat Case Study: How Can a Wellness Company Play It Smart?

*Analyzing Fitbit activity and sleep data using Python to identify potential marketing opportunities for Bellabeat.*

## Project Overview

This case study explores Fitbit activity and sleep data to better understand consumer wellness habits. The goal is to identify patterns that could help Bellabeat develop marketing strategies for its Leaf wellness tracker.

Using Python, pandas, Matplotlib, and Jupyter Notebook, I analyzed daily activity levels, sleep duration, and consistency in user behavior to develop three data-driven marketing recommendations.

## Key Findings

- **Daily Activity:** Participants averaged 7,638 steps per day, with 32% of recorded days falling below 5,000 steps.
- **Activity Levels:** 18 of the 33 participants were classified in the moderate activity group.
- **Consistency:** Approximately 27% of participants showed high variability in daily steps, suggesting opportunities to encourage more consistent activity habits.
- **Sleep Duration:** About 44% of recorded nights included fewer than seven hours of sleep.
- **Sleep Efficiency:** Average sleep efficiency was approximately 92%, suggesting that sleep duration may be a more useful focus for wellness messaging than sleep efficiency alone.

These findings suggest potential opportunities for Bellabeat to encourage personalized activity goals, consistent wellness routines, and greater sleep awareness.
