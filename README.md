# Bellabeat Case Study: How Can a Wellness Company Play It Smart?

*Analyzing Fitbit activity and sleep data using Python to identify potential marketing opportunities for Bellabeat.*

## Project Overview

This case study explores Fitbit activity and sleep data to better understand consumer wellness habits. The goal is to identify patterns that could help Bellabeat develop marketing strategies for its Leaf wellness tracker.

Using Python, pandas, Matplotlib, and Jupyter Notebook, I analyzed daily activity levels, sleep duration, and consistency in user behavior to develop three data-driven marketing recommendations.

## Key Findings

- **Daily Activity:** Participants averaged 7,638 steps per day, with 32% of recorded days falling below 5,000 steps.
- **Activity Levels:** 18 of the 33 participants were classified in the moderate activity group.
- **Consistency:** Approximately 27% of participants showed high variability in daily steps, suggesting opportunities to encourage more consistent activity habits.
- **Sleep Duration:** About 44% of recorded nights included fewer than seven hours of sleep.
- **Sleep Efficiency:** Average sleep efficiency was approximately 92%, suggesting that sleep duration may be a more useful focus for wellness messaging than sleep efficiency alone.

These findings suggest potential opportunities for Bellabeat to encourage personalized activity goals, consistent wellness routines, and greater sleep awareness.

## Marketing Recommendations

Based on my analysis, I developed three recommendations for Bellabeat:

### 1. Personalized Activity Goals
Encourage users to set realistic daily activity goals based on their individual habits rather than relying on a single step target for everyone. Personalized progress messages and achievable milestones could help users stay engaged.

### 2. Encourage Consistent Wellness Habits
Promote small, sustainable daily routines through reminders, progress tracking, and consistency-focused challenges. This approach could help users who struggle to maintain regular activity levels.

### 3. Promote Sleep Tracking and Awareness
Highlight the value of understanding sleep patterns and building healthier bedtime routines. Bellabeat could use educational content and personalized sleep insights to encourage greater awareness of sleep duration.

### Measuring Success
Bellabeat could test these strategies through targeted marketing campaigns and evaluate results using engagement rates, click-through rates, and conversion rates. A/B testing could help determine which messages resonate most with potential customers.

## Data Source and Limitations

**Dataset:** Fitbit Fitness Tracker Data  
**Source:** [Kaggle – Fitbit Fitness Tracker Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/fitbit)  
**License:** CC0 (Public Domain)  
**Collection Period:** April–May 2016

This analysis uses two datasets:
- `dailyActivity_merged.csv` — daily steps, activity levels, and calories.
- `sleepDay_merged.csv` — sleep duration and time spent in bed.

### Limitations

- The activity dataset includes 33 participants, while sleep data is available for only 24 participants.
- The data covers approximately one month in 2016, so findings may not reflect current consumer behavior.
- Participants recorded different amounts of activity and sleep data.
- The dataset does not specifically represent Bellabeat customers.
- Observed patterns are exploratory and do not establish cause-and-effect relationships.

These limitations mean the findings should be used to develop potential marketing strategies rather than make broad conclusions about all wellness device users.
